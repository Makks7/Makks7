### Research

**[pH-PINN](https://github.com/Makks7/pH-PINN)** · PyTorch, MATLAB/Simulink

Physics-informed surrogate for the Henson–Seborg pH neutralization benchmark, driving a closed-loop NMPC with output disturbance estimation. Test R² of 0.9962; offset-free tracking at 0.015 s/step, benchmarked against LSTM and linear MPC comparators.

**[PINN-gas-lift](https://github.com/Makks7/PINN-gas-lift)** · Julia, Flux.jl

Gas-lift allocation for Niger Delta wells. 20.1% efficiency gain, $3.5M/yr, 0.261s solve. Presented at SPE STSE 2026 (top 10 of 22 papers).

**[PINNs-for-isothermal-CSTR](https://github.com/Makks7/PINNs-for-isothermal-CSTR)** · PyTorch

Physics-constrained networks for isothermal reactor control. Setpoint tracking, real-time feasible.

**ML-RBI** · XGBoost, LightGBM

Risk-based inspection for 20,000 Niger Delta equipment records. AUC-ROC 0.858 under out-of-time validation, aligned to API 581.
